# Get started with Leantime

There are two ways to use Leantime. Only the first step differs: on Leantime Cloud you start a trial, and on your own server you install Leantime first. After that, everything works the same.

## On Leantime Cloud

1. **Start a free two-week trial** at [accounts.leantime.io](https://accounts.leantime.io/accounts/register/). Your workspace gets its own address, like yourteam.leantime.io.
2. **Sign in and look around.** Cloud includes the marketplace plugins and the AI features inside Leantime. To see which features are core and which come from a plugin, see [What can Leantime do?](https://docs.leantime.io/#/getting-started/features)
3. **Invite your team** from **Company → User Management** (see [User Management](using-leantime/user-management.md)).

To keep going after two weeks, add a card. If you don't, your workspace is paused and kept for 180 days, so you can come back to it. To pick it up again, or to have it removed sooner, email support@leantime.io. The [privacy policy](https://leantime.io/privacy/) has the details.

## On your own server

1. **Check what your server needs:** PHP 8.2 or later and MySQL 8.0 or MariaDB 10.6 or later (see [System requirements](installation/system-requirements.md)).
2. **Install Leantime.** With [Docker](installation/docker.md) it takes about 10 minutes. You can also install it [on a server with PHP and MySQL](installation/package-installation.md).
3. **Create the first account.** The first person to finish the installer becomes the Owner.
4. **Invite your team** from **Company → User Management** (see [User Management](using-leantime/user-management.md)). Sending invitations needs [email set up](installation/email-configuration.md).
5. **Optional: add plugins** from the [marketplace](https://marketplace.leantime.io/), for example Strategies and Programs, or Advanced Authentication for the mobile app (see [Plugins](installation/plugin-installation.md)).

The open source edition is free under the AGPLv3 license. You can move a self-hosted Leantime to Cloud later.

## What next?

Whichever you chose:

1. **Decide how to organize your work.** A few minutes here saves a lot of moving things around later; see [How should I organize my work in Leantime?](getting-started/organize-your-work.md)
2. **Set up your first project** (see [Projects](using-leantime/managing-projects.md#how-do-i-create-a-project-in-leantime)).
3. **Open My Work** to see your day: your to-dos from every project, and a calendar to plan your time (see [My Work Dashboard](using-leantime/my-work-dashboard.md)).
