# Organize research and academic work in Leantime

For research labs, PhD students, thesis writers and grant-funded studies: long projects with hard deadlines, many small steps, and a lot of reading and writing along the way. The setup below breaks the long stretch into outcomes you can see finishing.

## How should a research lab or thesis writer set up Leantime?

| Your situation | What to create |
|---|---|
| **One thesis, paper or course** | One project. A milestone per stage with a deadline, like "Literature review", "Data collection", "First draft" and "Submission". |
| **A lab with several studies** | A project per study, each with its own team. A [client](using-leantime/managing-clients.md) per funder or partner institution if their people need to see their studies. |
| **Several grants or research lines** | The Program Plans plugin groups the studies under each grant, so you see its milestones and progress together. |

## How do I break a thesis or paper into steps I can finish?

A paper is too big to feel progress on. Split each stage into outcomes small enough to finish in a few days: for a chapter, "Outline", "Sources gathered", "Draft", "Edits". Each one done is visible progress.

- **For your own work:** make each outcome a to-do and its steps subtasks.
- **For work you share:** make each outcome a small [milestone](using-leantime/roadmap-planning.md), so co-authors see it on the timeline.
- **For outcomes you report on,** like papers submitted or participants recruited, use a goal with a number and link it to the milestones that move it (see [Goals](using-leantime/goals.md)).

## Where do notes, protocols and sources go?

- **Docs** holds protocols, meeting notes and reading notes, and can show the milestone a doc belongs to (see [Docs](using-leantime/docs.md)).
- **Research boards** collect what you found and what it means (see [Research boards](using-leantime/research-boards.md)).
- **Files** keeps the PDFs and data files that belong to the project.

## Tips for research work

- **Plan your writing time in My Work:** drag a to-do onto the calendar to block the time for it (see [My Work Dashboard](using-leantime/my-work-dashboard.md)).
- **Supervisors and co-authors** who only read along can have the **Commenter** role.
- **Deadline moved?** Change the milestone's due date. When an unfinished stage is pushed past the end of the period, the Status Report lists it under **Changed this period**, which helps at supervision meetings. Dates moved within the period don't show there (see [Reports](using-leantime/reports.md)).
