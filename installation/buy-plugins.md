# Buy a plugin from the Leantime Marketplace

On your own server, you add paid plugins by buying a license on the [Leantime Marketplace](https://marketplace.leantime.io/) and installing the plugin with its license key. This page explains what you buy, how bundles and their keys work, and what the yearly renewal is for.

On Leantime Cloud, there's nothing to buy: the marketplace plugins are already included (see [Plugins](installation/using-plugins.md)).

## What am I buying?

**A license you keep.** You pay once for a plugin, for the number of users you choose, and the license is yours. It keeps working for as long as the plugin supports your version of Leantime.

**One year of updates and support.** Your purchase includes plugin updates and email support for the first year. After that, updates and support continue with a yearly plan (see [Do I have to pay every year?](#do-i-have-to-pay-every-year)).

## Which do I need: a plugin or a bundle?

- **A plugin** comes in user tiers, from **Personal (1–3 users)** up to **Enterprise (101–250 users)**. Choose the tier that covers everyone who's active in your Leantime, not only the people who use the plugin (see [What counts as a user for a plugin license?](installation/plugin-licenses.md#what-counts-as-a-user-for-a-plugin-license)).
- **A bundle** gets you several plugins for one team size, in one purchase. Each bundle's page lists the plugins in it.

## How do I buy a plugin?

1. **Find the plugin.** In Leantime, open **Company → Leantime Apps**, click **Learn More** on a plugin and then **Get a license**. Or go straight to [marketplace.leantime.io](https://marketplace.leantime.io/).
2. **Check that it supports your Leantime version.** The plugin's page shows the versions it needs, for example "For Leantime v3.13.0 & up". Your version is in Leantime's help menu (the question mark at the top right), under **System**.
3. **Choose your user tier** and click **Buy Now** (or **Add to Cart** for a bundle), then check out.
4. **Look for the email** with your order receipt and your license key. Use the email address you bought with to find it later.
5. **Install the plugin** with the key: see [Installing from the Marketplace](installation/plugin-installation.md#installing-from-the-marketplace).

## I bought a bundle. Which key do I use?

**Each plugin in a bundle has its own license key, and each key only installs its own plugin.** Install the plugins one at a time:

1. In **Company → Leantime Apps**, open the plugin's own card, not the bundle's. A bundle's card has no **Install** button.
2. Paste the key for that plugin, and click **Install**.
3. Repeat for the next plugin, with its key.

A key for the bundle as a whole doesn't install anything. If Leantime doesn't accept a key, check that it's the one for the plugin you're installing.

## Do I have to pay every year?

No. Your license is a one-time purchase, and you can keep using it with no obligation to continue.

What renews every year is the **updates and support plan**:

- **With the plan,** you get plugin updates, including updates for new Leantime versions, and email support.
- **Without it,** the plugin keeps working on the Leantime versions it supports. We can't guarantee that it works with Leantime versions released after your plan ends. When you update Leantime, a plugin that doesn't support the new version shows **Not compatible** and is switched off until you update it (see [What happens to plugins when I update Leantime?](installation/plugin-licenses.md#what-happens-to-plugins-when-i-update-leantime)).

Because of how the marketplace works, the plan is set to renew automatically. You can cancel the renewal in your marketplace account at any time, even right after you buy. Cancelling doesn't take away your license.

The plan also helps keep the open source Leantime project running and maintained.

## How do I add users or move to a bigger tier?

Upgrade in your account on the marketplace. You get credit for what you paid before, so you only pay for the step up.

## Where do I find my license key later?

In the email with your order receipt. If you can't find it, enter the email address you bought with on the [Lost License](https://marketplace.leantime.io/lost-license/) page, and the marketplace sends your license and receipt again. If your email address has changed, [contact the marketplace](https://marketplace.leantime.io/contact/).

Inside Leantime, the key is stored with the plugin, so updates don't ask for it again.

## What if the plugin doesn't work?

- **The key isn't accepted:** see [License Key Not Recognized](installation/plugin-installation.md#license-key-not-recognized), and for bundles, [Which key do I use?](#i-bought-a-bundle-which-key-do-i-use)
- **The plugin shows Not compatible:** update the plugin, or check its supported versions (see [What happens to plugins when I update Leantime?](installation/plugin-licenses.md#what-happens-to-plugins-when-i-update-leantime)).
- **The plugin was switched off:** too many active users for the license (see [What happens when we have more users than the license allows?](installation/plugin-licenses.md#what-happens-when-we-have-more-users-than-the-license-allows)), or it kept causing errors (see [What happens when a plugin crashes?](installation/plugin-licenses.md#what-happens-when-a-plugin-crashes)).
- **Anything else:** see [Where can I get help with a plugin?](installation/plugin-licenses.md#where-can-i-get-help-with-a-plugin)
