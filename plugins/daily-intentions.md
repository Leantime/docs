# Daily Intentions

A plan to "work on the report tomorrow" is easy to put off. A plan that says when you'll start and what you'll do first is much easier to follow. The Daily Intentions plugin helps you make that kind of plan for your to-dos, right on your My Work dashboard.

## What does it add?

- **A Daily Intention widget** for My Work, with a card for yesterday, today and the next three days. See [How do I set a daily intention?](using-leantime/my-work-dashboard.md#how-do-i-set-a-daily-intention)
- **A plan in one sentence:** the to-do, the moment you'll start (like after lunch), and the first step. See [How do I write an intention that works?](using-leantime/my-work-dashboard.md#how-do-i-write-an-intention-that-works)

## Do I have it on Leantime Cloud?

Yes. Daily Intentions is already on in every Leantime Cloud workspace. It isn't available for self-hosted Leantime.

## Why does it help?

Daily Intentions are based on implementation intentions, also called if-then plans: "When situation X comes, I'll do Y." Deciding the moment and the action ahead of time links the two, so when the moment comes, you act without having to decide again.

A review of 94 studies found that if-then plans helped people reach their goals more often than goals alone. They've also been studied for focus and self-control in children with ADHD. Leantime's version fits the plan to the to-dos you already have: the to-do, the moment, and the first step.

For more, read [How to use implementation intentions to reach your goals](https://leantime.io/how-to-use-implementation-intentions-to-reach-your-goals/) on the Leantime blog.
