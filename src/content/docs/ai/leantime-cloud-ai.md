Leantime Cloud has an AI assistant built in, called **L.E.O.** It works inside Leantime, with your projects and your permissions: you can ask it questions, have it sort your to-dos, break a to-do into steps, or help you write.

L.E.O. is only in Leantime Cloud. If you host Leantime yourself, or you'd rather use the AI assistant you already have, connect it through the [MCP server](/installation/leantime-mcp/) instead.

## Which should I use: L.E.O. or my own assistant?

| If you… | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Want help right where you work, with nothing to set up | **L.E.O.** (Leantime Cloud) |
| Already use Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or GitHub Copilot every day, and want it to see and update your Leantime work | **Your own assistant**, through the [MCP server](/installation/leantime-mcp/) |
| Host Leantime yourself | **Your own assistant**, through the [MCP server](/installation/leantime-mcp/) |

You can use both. They do similar things: both can find your to-dos, create and change them, log time and write updates, and both act with your permissions only.

## How do I ask L.E.O. something?

1. **Click the L.E.O. button** at the bottom right of any page, or **L.E.O.** in the menu. The chat opens on the right; the expand button makes it full screen.
2. **Ask in your own words.** For example:
   - "What should I focus on today?"
   - "Create a to-do in the Website project to review the homepage copy, due Friday."
   - "Which milestones in the Eastside pantry project are due this month?"
   - "Log 2 hours on #456 for yesterday."

L.E.O. can find and change to-dos, milestones and goals, plan your day on your calendar, log time and post project status updates. It only sees the projects you can see.

L.E.O. is told to check with you before it changes anything, but it doesn't always. Read what it says it will do, and say "show me first" for bigger changes.

## How do I let L.E.O. sort my to-dos?

On your [My Work dashboard](/using-leantime/my-work-dashboard/):

1. **Click the magic wand** at the top of the **My ToDos** widget, next to the group button.
2. **Switch on Let L.E.O. prioritize tasks.**
3. **Pick a Prioritization Mode:**
   - **Reprioritize:** sorts all your to-dos.
   - **Top 3 Focus:** shows only the three to work on first.

L.E.O. looks at due dates, priority and the size of each to-do, and mixes small to-dos with a bigger one so it's easier to get started. Next to each to-do, the info button explains why it's where it is.

The order is worked out once a day. To work it out again, for example after you've added to-dos, click **Reprioritize my Tasks** in the same menu. While L.E.O. sorts your list, you can't drag to-dos or group them yourself; switch it off to get your own order back.

## How do I break a to-do into steps?

Open the to-do and click **Break Down Task**, in the subtasks area or under **AI Tools** in the to-do's ⋮ menu. L.E.O. adds subtasks right away, each with an effort, planned hours and a priority. Delete any you don't need.

## How do I get help writing?

In any editor, like a to-do's description or a doc, click **AI Tools** in the toolbar, or type `/ai`. Paste or type your text, choose what you'd like, and L.E.O. writes it:

- **Summarize**, **Proofread**
- **Rewrite to be friendlier**, **Rewrite to be more professional**
- **Structure Meeting Notes**, **Extract Action Items**
- **Brainstorm Solutions**, **Create Implementation Intention**
- **Write a blog post about**

## What does it cost?

L.E.O. uses AI credits from your Leantime Cloud plan. Chatting, sorting, breaking down and writing each use some. If you run out, L.E.O. tells you; you can buy more credits or change your plan (see [Leantime Cloud pricing](https://leantime.io/pricing/)).
