Plugins add features to Leantime. Each one does one job, like planning a strategy above your projects, sketching on a whiteboard, or repeating a to-do every week. Pick the ones your team needs.

- **In Leantime Cloud,** the marketplace plugins are already switched on, except Advanced Authentication, which is for self-hosted servers.
- **On your own server,** get a plugin from the [Leantime Marketplace](https://marketplace.leantime.io/) and install it with its license key: see [Installing plugins](/installation/plugin-installation/) and [Plugin licenses and updates](/installation/plugin-licenses/).

## Which plugin do I need?

### Plan above your projects

- **[Strategy Pro](/plugins/strategy-pro/):** set out what your organization is working toward, with focus areas and measurable goals, and see which programs and projects serve them. [How to use strategies](/using-leantime/strategies/).
- **[Programs](/plugins/programs/):** group related projects into a program, and see their to-dos, milestones and people in one place. [How to use programs](/using-leantime/programs/).

### Think it through and take notes

- **[Whiteboards](/plugins/whiteboards/):** sketch floor plans, flows and diagrams on a shared whiteboard inside a project. [How to use whiteboards](/using-leantime/whiteboards/).
- **[Notes](/plugins/notes/):** jot down quick notes and ideas that don't belong in a project's docs yet. [How to use Notes](/using-leantime/notes/).

### Add to your to-dos

- **[Custom fields](/plugins/custom-fields/):** add your own fields to to-dos, for the details your team tracks that Leantime doesn't have a field for. [How to use custom fields](/using-leantime/custom-fields/).
- **[Recurring to-dos](/plugins/recurring-todos/):** create a to-do again on a schedule, like every week or every month. [How to make a to-do repeat](/using-leantime/task-management/#how-do-i-make-a-to-do-repeat).

### Stay focused

- **[Pomodoro timer](/plugins/pomodoro/):** work in focused blocks with short breaks in between. [How to use the timer](/using-leantime/my-work-dashboard/#how-do-i-use-the-pomodoro-timer).
- **[Daily Intentions](/plugins/daily-intentions/):** decide when you'll start a to-do and what the first step is. Leantime Cloud only. [How to set a daily intention](/using-leantime/my-work-dashboard/#how-do-i-set-a-daily-intention).

### Sign in and connect other tools

- **[Advanced Authentication](/plugins/advanced-authentication/):** sign in with Google, Microsoft, GitHub, Okta and more, and use the [Leantime Mobile](https://leantime.io/mobile) app, including phone notifications (see [Set up the mobile app](/installation/mobile-app-setup/) and [Leantime on your phone](/using-leantime/mobile-app/)). Self-hosted only.
- **[MCP Server](/installation/leantime-mcp/):** let AI assistants like Claude work with your Leantime projects.

## How do I switch a plugin on?

On your own server, go to **Company → Leantime Apps → My Apps** and switch the plugin on. Its menu items and options appear right away. See [Installing plugins](/installation/plugin-installation/) for the license key and the first install.

Some plugins add their own items to the menu, like **Whiteboards** in a project. Others add options to screens you already know, like an extra field in a to-do.
